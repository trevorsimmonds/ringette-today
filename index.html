Ringette Today

Ringette schedules for the teams you follow

Ringette Today shows the games for the teams you pick: times, rinks, scores as they come in, and who's on the same ice right before and after.

It covers the NCRRL and GAARA leagues, the LERQ (Quebec AA league), Ringette Ontario tournaments, Quebec tournaments on Planitournoi and the Fredericton Early Bird tournament. Free, no account, no ads, no tracking.

Ringette Today needs JavaScript turned on.

Only NCRRL, GAARA and LERQ league games and Ringette Ontario and Quebec tournament games are listed — other groups may also be using this ice.
